    How it works
    1. You get a code. The server reserves a free four-digit code for your drop.
    2. Your browser encrypts. Everything is sealed with AES-256-GCM under a key derived from the code (PBKDF2, 600k rounds) and a server-side secret. Only ciphertext is uploaded.
    3. They enter the code. Their browser downloads the ciphertext and decrypts it locally. Storage never sees your content or your code.

    Four digits are quick to share but also quick to guess, so drops expire, can burn after one open, and wrong guesses are rate-limited. Use it for things that matter for minutes, not secrets that must stay secret forever.
